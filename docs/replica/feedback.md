# Feedback: what Jobright users say

> Produced following the `/replica-entrepreneur` method, done by hand. Reviews are paraphrased with links; nothing is invented.
> **Sample size: ~20 individual Trustpilot reviews (1–3★, Sep 2026) + 5 third-party review articles summarizing Reddit/Trustpilot/G2.** Below the skill's 100+ target, so treat the themes as directional.
> Ratings are inconsistent across sources (Trustpilot shows 4.8 overall; one roundup cites 2.9). That suggests heavy solicited positive reviews and a real negative tail.

## Themes (ranked by how often they appeared)

| # | Theme | Count / strength | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No results / no insight into why**: months of use or hundreds of apps → few interviews | 4 reviews | [Trustpilot 1–3★](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/jobright.ai?stars=1&stars=2&stars=3) |
| 2 | **AI resume / cover letter quality**: weak suggestions, bad formatting, invented metrics or skills | 4 reviews + roundups | Trustpilot; [jobhire](https://jobhire.ai/blog/jobright-ai-review-and-decision-guide-2026); [adzuna](https://www.adzuna.co.uk/blog/jobright-review-better-alternative-in-2025/) |
| 3 | **Autofill unreliable**: wrong data entered; one user said only a small fraction worked | 2 reviews + roundups | Trustpilot; [scoutify](https://scoutify.com/blog/jobright-review) |
| 4 | **Bad listings**: duplicates, expired or "phantom" jobs, can't filter unwanted titles | 3 reviews + roundups | Trustpilot; adzuna |
| 5 | **Price / billing**: too expensive, price rose, auto-renew and cancellation friction | 1 review + roundups | Trustpilot; [favtutor](https://favtutor.com/jobright-ai-review/) |
| 6 | **Bugs / support**: extension bugs, alerts mismatched, support didn't respond | 2 reviews | Trustpilot |
| 7 | Tracker limitations (mentioned, not detailed) | weak | App Store summary |

What people **like**: clean UI, match scores, fresh postings surfaced early, time saved on discovery.

## Three lists

**What users hate**
- AI that confidently produces wrong output (hallucinated resume bullets, wrong autofill)
- Paying for months with nothing to show and no explanation
- Junk listings (duplicates, expired)

**Missing features**
- A way to see *why* the search isn't working (funnel by source, role, time)
- Filtering out unwanted titles
- Reliable, verifiable automation

**Unsolved problems**
- "Am I being ghosted, or is it just slow?"
- "Which of my channels (referral, LinkedIn, ATS) actually gets replies?"
